01   What You Are Looking At
🎯
Purpose of the Dashboard
One view. All signals. Zero guesswork.
The OpsReview Dashboard is a monthly IT operations scorecard. It shows how the SAP and Non-ERP application support team is performing across:
  • Ticket volume and service quality
  • SLA compliance and resolution speed
  • Change and problem management health
  • Project delivery and team capacity
💡
What You Do Not Need to Know
This is built for business readers, not technologists.
No technical background is required. Every number is a business-readable metric with a colour indicator. You cannot accidentally change any data by clicking — the dashboard is entirely read-only for viewers.

The data is refreshed by the operations team before every governance review. What you see is the approved version for that period.
02   The Colour Code (RAG)

Every metric on the dashboard uses the same three colours. The scale never changes between sections or tabs.

Green — On Target
No action needed
Acknowledge and move on. Performance is within contracted limits.
Amber — Warning
Approaching the limit
Ask the team: "What is the recovery plan and timeline?"
Red — Critical
Target has been missed
Requires a named owner and a committed due date — today, in this meeting.
03   The Pulse Banner
⚑
Read this first — every time.
At the top of every dashboard page is a highlighted banner called the Pulse or Key Flag. It is written by the operations team before the meeting to draw immediate attention to the single most important event, risk, or achievement of the period. It sets the tone for the entire review. Do not skip it.
04   The Seven Tabs — What Each One Tells You
📊
Overview
"How are we doing overall this month?"
Four headline numbers at the top of this tab:
  • SAP Total Volume — tickets across all SAP systems. Delta arrow shows MoM direction.
  • SAP SLA Composite — are service commitments being met? ≥ 98% = green.
  • Non-ERP Total Volume — same, for non-SAP systems.
  • Non-ERP SLA Composite — SLA performance for non-SAP.

Two 6-month trend charts below help spot demand risk (rising volume) or throughput risk (growing backlog).
Key question: "Is the backlog growing or shrinking?"
💻
SAP Modules
"Which SAP system needs attention?"
Click any module (CRM, SCM, FICO, etc.) to drill in. Each shows:

INC / SR SplitProblems vs tasks — high INC = instability
Net FlowPositive = backlog shrinking ✓
Response SLA %Acknowledged on time?
Resolution SLA %Fixed on time?
MTTR (hours)How long did fixes take?

Also shows open change requests (RFaC) and Problem Records aging. Old open problems = risk signal.
Key question: "Which module has the lowest SLA and what happened?"
🔧
Non-SAP Modules
"Same view, for non-SAP systems."
Identical layout to SAP Modules, covering: ACE, CPQ, Democenter, Corp Sol, HR, IAM, M365, and SerNow.

All the same KPIs apply — received vs baseline, SLA compliance, MTTR, priority breakdown, age distribution, RFaC and Problem Record aging.
Key question: "Are Non-ERP modules keeping pace with SAP performance?"
🎯
Quality & MTTR
"Are we fixing things properly the first time?"
MetricGood =
MTTRLower (< 20 hrs = green)
Reopen Rate %Lower (< 3% = green)
First-Fix Rate %Higher (> 85% = green)
SLA Near-MissesWatch for upward trend
SLA BreachesZero is the target

A high reopen rate with a low MTTR often means tickets are being closed too quickly without a proper fix.
Key question: "Which modules have the highest reopen rate and what is being done?"
📋
Action Items
"What did we agree to do, and is it getting done?"
  • Active Action Log — every open commitment with owner and due date. Overdue items must be challenged.
  • Leadership Escalations — issues needing senior intervention. Nothing here should be a surprise.
  • Appreciations — positive recognition received. Worth naming aloud.
  • Service Offers / CCRs — additional work beyond contracted scope, proposed or approved.
Key question: "How many items are overdue and who is accountable?"
🚀
Projects & CI
"Are our major projects on track?"
Each project card shows:
  • RAG Overall — health in one colour.
  • Status Phase — Explore → Prepare → Realize → Deploy.
  • Planned Go-Live — the target date.
  • Status Narrative — what happened this period.

The Continuous Improvement section lists automation and process improvements, including how much effort they are saving the team each month.
Key question: "Which projects are RED and what decision is needed to unblock them?"
👥
Headcount
"Is the team sized right for the volume of work?"
FTE counts for: SAP AMS, SAP Projects, Non-ERP AMS, Non-ERP Projects.

The bar chart shows tickets per FTE per module:
  • Green — ≤ 35 tickets/FTE — sustainable
  • Amber — ≤ 45 tickets/FTE — approaching overload
  • Red — > 45 tickets/FTE — under pressure, quality risk
Key question: "Which modules are running hot and is there a staffing plan?"

✅
What NOT to Worry About
  • The dashboard is read-only. You cannot accidentally change any data by clicking around.
  • Data is refreshed by the operations team before every governance review. What you see is approved.
  • If a metric shows "N/A", it means data was not available for that item — not that something went wrong.
  • A delta arrow (↑ ↓) shows change versus last month. Red ↑ on volume = more tickets arrived. Red ↓ on SLA = performance dropped.
06   Five Questions Every Review Should Answer
1
Volume Is ticket volume trending up, down, or stable? Is the backlog growing or being cleared?
2
SLA Are we meeting our contractual commitments? Which modules are amber or red, and why?
3
Quality Are fixes sticking? Is the reopen rate low and the first-fix rate high?
4
Actions Are all agreed actions on track? Who owns the overdue ones and what is the new date?
5
Projects Are projects on schedule and on budget? What decisions or unblocking actions are needed from leadership today?
07   Glossary — Plain English Definitions
INC / IncidentAn unplanned interruption or degradation of a service
SR / Service RequestA standard request — e.g. access, change, new setup
SLAService Level Agreement — the contractual deadline for responding to or resolving a ticket
MTTRMean Time to Resolve — average time from ticket creation to resolution (in hours)
RFaC / RFCRequest for Change — a formal request to make a planned change to a system
PRB / ProblemProblem Record — a root-cause investigation for recurring or major incidents
Net FlowResolved minus Received — positive means the backlog is shrinking
FTEFull-Time Equivalent — one person working full time
MoMMonth-over-Month — the change compared to the previous month
RAGRed / Amber / Green — the traffic-light status system used throughout the dashboard
BaselineThe contracted expected volume of tickets per module per month
Near-MissA ticket that met its SLA deadline but only just — within 10% of the limit
CCRCommercial Change Request — a change to the scope or cost of the contract
CIContinuous Improvement — an ongoing initiative to improve efficiency or quality
AMSApplication Managed Services — the ongoing support and maintenance workstream
CWCalendar Week — used to stamp the reporting period (e.g. CW22 = 22nd week of the year)
Reopen Rate% of resolved tickets that were reopened because the fix did not hold
First-Fix Rate% of tickets resolved correctly on the first attempt without reopening
BacklogThe number of tickets that remain open and unresolved at the end of the period
Pulse / Key FlagA one-sentence highlight written by the operations team — the most important event of the period
